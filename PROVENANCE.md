# Skill provenance

The portable customer skills reconcile the existing first-party product workflow packages with [Soxoa Claude plugins at 2bc7d09d](https://github.com/johnarndt/soxoa-claude-plugins/tree/2bc7d09dbae17735ddfd008eeaf75bba1b02e4af). That immutable source has 28 customer skills and 15 separate operations skills. Narrow tool variants are consolidated into outcomes, and five additional active products are covered here.

Current public endpoint schemas were inspected on 27 October 2026. Postedly and Dibatto protect their catalogs, so their richer contracts were checked against maintained first-party source and existing client metadata. Static validation, tool coverage and instruction review do not establish authenticated customer acceptance, paid processing accuracy or fulfillment.

Both distribution sources are first-party Soxoa LLC materials licensed under MIT. Hosted application source and customer data are not distributed.
