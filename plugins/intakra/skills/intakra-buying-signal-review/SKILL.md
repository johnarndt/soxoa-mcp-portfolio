# Intakra buying-signal review

Use the public Intakra tools for caller-supplied, unverified evidence.

- Use `score_buying_signal_evidence` to calculate fit, timing, evidence-quality, and priority scores.
- Use `prepare_why_now_review` to create a review-only brief with evidence gaps and unsupported-claim flags.
- Preserve source labels, dates, and uncertainty.
- Do not claim that Intakra opened sources, researched the account, accessed a private workspace, or verified the evidence.
- Never infer allocated budget, decision authority, purchase probability, or buying intent unless supported; retain the tool's human-review requirement.

When the evidence is unstructured, use `references/evidence-fields.md` to map supplied notes to dated account, fit and signal fields. When fit criteria are missing, use `references/fit-criteria.md` to prepare proposed rules for the user to confirm. Do not manufacture missing evidence or claim to have opened a pasted link without actually reading it through an authorized tool. For several supplied accounts, score them consistently and preserve per-account gaps rather than treating a ranking as buyer intent.
